# Urban Encroachment Analysis Using GIS and Remote Sensing

*A geospatial assessment of urban development and its impact on archaeological sites in Gujarat, India.*

---

## Overview

This project evaluates the impact of urban development on archaeological sites across Gujarat using Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Remote Sensing techniques. Rapid urban expansion, infrastructure development, and land-use transformation can alter site integrity, increase development pressure, and contribute to the degradation of cultural landscapes.

The analysis integrates Land Use Land Cover (LULC) mapping and spatial analysis to identify archaeological sites affected by urban growth and built-up area expansion.

This work was conducted as part of my PhD research at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN).

---

## Objectives

- Map urban and built-up land use across Gujarat.
- Identify archaeological sites affected by urban expansion.
- Assess the spatial relationship between urban growth and cultural heritage locations.
- Evaluate urban development as an anthropogenic threat to archaeological resources.
- Support evidence-based heritage management and conservation planning.

---

## Study Area

The study covers archaeological sites distributed across Gujarat, India, a region that has experienced significant urbanization and infrastructure development over recent decades.

---

## Methodology

The analysis was carried out using GIS and Remote Sensing techniques including:

- Land Use Land Cover (LULC) Classification
- Spatial Overlay Analysis
- Buffer Analysis
- Proximity Assessment
- Encroachment Assessment
- Spatial Statistics

---

## Land Use Land Cover (LULC) Classification

![LULC Classification](Images/01-lulc-classification.png)

The LULC map was used to identify built-up areas and evaluate the spatial relationship between urban development and archaeological site locations. The analysis enabled the assessment of development pressure and potential encroachment risks affecting cultural heritage resources.

---

## Results

The assessment identified archaeological sites exposed to varying levels of urban development pressure. Spatial analysis highlighted areas where expanding built-up land and infrastructure have increased the risk of encroachment and landscape transformation.

The results demonstrate how GIS-based land use analysis can support heritage risk assessment, monitoring, and conservation planning.
